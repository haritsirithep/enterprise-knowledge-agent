# Expense Reimbursement Policy (Nimbus Co., Ltd.)

## 1. How to submit a claim
1. Log in to the ExpenseHub portal.
2. Click "New Claim" and choose the expense category.
3. Attach a photo or scan of every receipt.
4. Submit within 30 calendar days of the expense date. Late claims are rejected.

## 2. Receipts
- A receipt is required for any expense above 200 THB.
- If a receipt is lost, fill in the "Lost Receipt Declaration" form in ExpenseHub and get your manager's signature. Maximum 1,000 THB per lost receipt.

## 3. Approval limits
- Up to 10,000 THB: approved by your direct manager.
- Above 10,000 THB: approved by your manager and the department director.

## 4. Taxi and transport
- Taxi or ride-hailing is reimbursable for client visits, or when you work after 22:00 or before 06:00.
- Maximum 600 THB per trip. Normal commuting between home and office is not reimbursable.

## 5. Meals during business travel
- Domestic travel: per diem of 300 THB per day, no receipt needed.
- International travel: per diem of 50 USD per day.

## 6. Payment
- Approved claims are paid with the next payroll on the 25th of each month.
- Claims approved after the 15th are paid in the following month's payroll.
