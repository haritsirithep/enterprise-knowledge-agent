# IT Support Guide (Nimbus Co., Ltd.)

## 1. Password reset
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- Reset your password yourself at the Self-Service Password portal (sspr.nimbus.local).
- After 5 wrong attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. If it is still locked, call the IT Helpdesk at extension 1234.

## 2. VPN for working from home
- Install "Nimbus VPN" from the Company Portal app.
- Log in with your company email and approve the MFA prompt on your phone.

## 3. IT tickets
- Open a ticket in ServiceDesk for any IT problem. Normal tickets get a response within 4 working hours.
- For urgent problems that stop your work, call extension 1234.

## 4. Lost or stolen laptop
- Report to IT within 24 hours by calling extension 1234, so IT can lock the device remotely.
