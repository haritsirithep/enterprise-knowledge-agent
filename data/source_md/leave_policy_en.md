# Leave Policy (Nimbus Co., Ltd.)

## 1. Annual leave
- 10 working days in your first year. +1 day for each year of service, up to 15 days.
- Request in the PeopleNow HR portal at least 3 working days in advance. Your manager approves it.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over, but must be used by 31 March of the next year.

## 2. Sick leave
- Up to 30 paid working days per year.
- Inform your manager before 09:00 on the day you are sick.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days. Upload it in PeopleNow within 3 days of returning to work.

## 3. Personal leave
- 3 paid days per year for personal business (for example, government paperwork or family matters).
- Request at least 1 working day in advance, except in emergencies.

## 4. Maternity leave
- 98 days in total, including weekends and holidays, according to Thai labour law.

## 5. Unpaid leave
- Needs approval from your manager and HR. Maximum 30 days per year.
